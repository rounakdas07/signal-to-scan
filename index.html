What the interval measures

The clock starts at a positive blood result and ends at the event each paper recorded. A clinical diagnosis, a first positive scan and a scan ordered because of the blood result answer different questions. Sampling frequency also affects the interval.

Which evidence is included?

Six selected primary studies of follow-up after treatment for breast cancer, reviewed on 12 November 2026. Each reports confirmed recurrence with a blood-test result or staging after a blood-test alert. Studies of progression in known metastatic disease fall outside this review. This is a curated selection. Numbers stay within their original cohorts.

Counting days

For GenoCTC and EBLIS, the papers give individual intervals. Pick a minimum to count documented intervals at least that long. Every observed recurrence stays in the denominator, including undetected cases and positive results without a usable date. Each study card identifies the endpoint.

Why this matters for Frontier

Frontier is developing Sentinel qCTC to count intact circulating tumour cells. The whole-cell studies are the closest comparisons here. The tumour DNA studies track a different signal. All six evaluated other assays. A direct Sentinel study would need the dates of blood draws and scans, how each work-up started, and results for people who relapsed with a negative blood test.
